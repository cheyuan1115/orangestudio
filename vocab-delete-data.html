如何刪除帳號與資料

「學測單字+」App（開發者：Orange Studio）提供 App 內建的自助刪除功能，步驟如下：

  1. 開啟「學測單字+」App 並登入您的帳號
  2. 點選首頁右上角的「設定」
  3. 捲動到「帳號」區塊，點選「刪除帳號」
  4. 依畫面指示完成兩次確認

會刪除哪些資料

確認後，以下資料將立即永久刪除，且無法復原：

  • 帳號資料（Email、暱稱、使用者 ID、好友代碼）
  • 雲端備份的學習進度（單字學習紀錄、測驗成績、經驗值 XP、連續學習天數）
  • 好友關係、封鎖清單、鼓勵訊息紀錄
  • 裝置推播權杖
  • 裝置本機儲存的學習資料

刪除作業沒有額外保留期限，資料刪除後立即從我們的伺服器移除。

訂閱相關

刪除帳號不會自動取消您向 Apple／Google 訂閱的付費項目，請另至 App Store 或 Google Play 的訂閱管理頁面取消，以避免持續扣款。

English

How to Delete Your Account and Data

學測單字+ (Vocab+), developed by Orange Studio, offers a self-service deletion option built into the app:

  1. Open the 學測單字+ app and sign in to your account
  2. Tap "Settings" (設定) in the top-right corner of the home screen
  3. Scroll to the "Account" (帳號) section and tap "Delete Account" (刪除帳號)
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm twice

What Gets Deleted

Once confirmed, the following data is permanently deleted immediately and cannot be recovered:

  • Account data (email, display name, user ID, friend code)
  • Cloud-backed learning progress (vocabulary study records, quiz scores, XP, study streak)
  • Friend connections, blocked list, encouragement message history
  • Device push notification token
  • Locally stored study data on your device

There is no additional retention period — data is removed from our servers immediately upon deletion.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel any active Apple or Google subscription. Please cancel separately via the App Store or Google Play subscription management page to avoid continued billing.

Need Help?

If you're unable to complete the steps above, or would like us to process the deletion on your behalf, email cheyuan1115@gmail.com with the email address associated with your account, and we will assist promptly.
