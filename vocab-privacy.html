概述

Orange Studio（以下簡稱「我們」）重視您的隱私。本政策說明「學測單字+」如何處理您的資料。

我們收集的資料

使用本 App 需要建立帳號（Email/密碼、Apple 登入或 Google 登入其中一種），我們會收集並儲存以下資料：

  • 帳號資料：Email 地址、暱稱、系統產生的使用者 ID
  • 學習進度：單字學習紀錄、測驗成績、連續學習天數、經驗值（XP）
  • 好友功能資料：好友代碼、好友清單、封鎖清單、排行榜互動（如互相鼓勵訊息）
  • 裝置推播權杖（Push Token）：用於發送學習提醒與好友動態通知
  • 訂閱資料：透過 RevenueCat 處理的訂閱狀態（不含您的付款卡號等敏感資訊，付款由 Apple/Google 官方金流處理）

資料儲存與傳輸

帳號與學習進度儲存於 Google Firebase（Firestore、Authentication）伺服器，並透過加密連線（HTTPS/TLS）傳輸。部分學習資料同時保留在您裝置本機，供離線使用。

資料分享對象

您的暱稱、經驗值與連續天數，會顯示給您加入好友的使用者（排行榜功能）。我們不會將您的個人資料出售或分享給第三方廣告商。

第三方服務

  • Google Firebase：帳號、資料庫、推播基礎建設，請參閱 Google 隱私權政策
  • RevenueCat：訂閱管理服務，請參閱 RevenueCat 隱私權政策
  • Apple / Google 帳號登入：僅用於身份驗證，請參閱各自的隱私權政策

刪除帳號與資料

您可以隨時在 App 內「設定 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」永久刪除您的帳號與所有雲端資料，詳見 資料刪除說明。

English

Overview

Orange Studio ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how 學測單字+ (Vocab+) handles your data.

Data We Collect

Using this app requires creating an account (Email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In). We collect and store:

  • Account data: email address, display name, system-generated user ID
  • Learning progress: vocabulary study records, quiz scores, study streak, XP
  • Friends feature data: friend code, friends list, blocked list, leaderboard interactions (e.g. encouragement messages)
  • Device push token: used to send study reminders and friend activity notifications
  • Subscription data via RevenueCat: subscription status (not your payment card details — payments are processed by Apple/Google directly)

Storage and Transmission

Account and progress data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication) and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Some study data is also kept locally on your device for offline use.

Who We Share Data With

Your display name, XP, and streak are visible to users you've connected with as friends (leaderboard feature). We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: account, database, and push infrastructure. See Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: subscription management. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple / Google Sign-In: used only for authentication. See their respective privacy policies

Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all cloud data at any time via Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app. See Data Deletion Instructions.
